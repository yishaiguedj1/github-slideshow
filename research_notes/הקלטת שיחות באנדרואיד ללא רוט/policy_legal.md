# Google Play Policy and Legal Constraints for an Android Call-Recording App (as of Oct 2026)

Research note: direct fetches of support.google.com, android-developers.googleblog.com, androidauthority.com and cubeacr.app failed with DNS errors from the research environment, so many findings below come from search-result summaries of those pages plus secondary coverage. Policy wording quoted is as reported. Verify against the live Play Policy Center before relying on exact text.

## 1. Google Play policy: Accessibility API, permission declarations, foreground service types, default-handler roles

### Takeaway
Since 11 May 2022 Google Play forbids using AccessibilityService for "remote call audio recording", and the IsAccessibilityTool flag is limited to apps built to help people with disabilities. Call Log permissions are limited to apps that are the default Phone, SMS or Assistant handler, and Android 14+ apps must declare each foreground service type in Play Console. In practice, the only fully compliant route on Play to capturing both sides of a cellular call is to be the default (and in practice preloaded or OEM) dialer. A third-party Play app can still offer manual or one-sided recording, VoIP recording or note-taking features.

### Cited Findings
- Policy text (2022, still the basis): "The Accessibility API is not designed and cannot be requested for remote call audio recording." Effective 11 May 2022, after a grace period of more than 30 days. Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/google-is-banning-third-party-call-recording-apps-from-the-play-store-093201443.html), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/new-google-play-store-policy-cracks-down-on-call-recording-apps), [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/googles-ban-on-call-recording-apps-to-come-into-effect-starting-today-333149-2022-05-11).
- IsAccessibilityTool: only "apps with a core functionality intended to directly support people with disabilities are eligible to use the IsAccessibilityTool" flag. Other apps using the API must meet the prominent disclosure and consent requirements of the User Data policy and document the API use in the Play listing. Source: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/google-is-banning-third-party-call-recording-apps-from-the-play-store-093201443.html), [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/google_to_kill_callrecording_apps_on_play_store_on_may_11-news-54039.php).
- Google's webinar defined "remote": "Remote in this context refers to call audio recording where the person on the other end is unaware of the recording is taking place." The same webinar reportedly suggested that apps with a disclaimer and consent from all parties might keep recording. Source: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/google-is-banning-third-party-call-recording-apps-from-the-play-store-093201443.html). This is ambiguous in practice: the major apps (Truecaller, NLL ACR) still removed Accessibility-based recording from their Play builds. Source: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/truecaller-call-recording-google-play-policy), [NLL](https://nllapps.com/apps/acr/future).
- Dialer exemption: "If the app is the default dialer on the phone and also pre-loaded, accessibility capability is not required to get access to the incoming audio stream" (Moun Choi, Google). Preinstalled system apps were unaffected. Source: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/google-is-banning-third-party-call-recording-apps-from-the-play-store-093201443.html), [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/google_to_kill_callrecording_apps_on_play_store_on_may_11-news-54039.php).
- SMS and Call Log permissions (READ_CALL_LOG and others) are high-risk. Permitted uses are default SMS handling, default Phone handling or Assistant handling. The app must be the active default handler before prompting and must stop using the permission once it is no longer the default. Apps that qualify, or claim an exception, must submit the Permissions Declaration Form in Play Console, and apps that do not qualify must remove the permissions from the manifest. Source: [Play Console Help 9047303](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9047303).
- Upcoming change: effective 27 January 2027, phone-call account verification will no longer be an allowed use of READ_CALL_LOG. Google points developers to the Digital Credentials API or the SMS Retriever API instead. Source: [Play Console Help 17225965](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17225965), [16558241](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16558241?hl=en).
- History: during the 2018/2019 Call Log enforcement, Google's reviewers treated call recording as a non-permitted use, and NLL's ACR lost access to phone numbers. Source: [NLL](https://www.nllapps.com/apps/acr/google-denies-phone-number-accesss.htm), [XDA](https://www.xda-developers.com/google-remove-unapproved-apps-use-call-log-sms-permissions/).
- Foreground services, Android 14+: each FGS type must be declared in the manifest along with its permission (for example FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE). Play Console requires a foreground service declaration under App content that describes the feature for each type and the user impact if the task were deferred. All FGS types are subject to review under the Device and Network Abuse policy. Source: [Play Console Help 13392821](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13392821), [Android Developers: declare FGS](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/fgs/declare?authuser=1).
- The phoneCall FGS type requires FOREGROUND_SERVICE_PHONE_CALL plus either MANAGE_OWN_CALLS or the ROLE_DIALER default-dialer role. It is intended for continuing an ongoing call via ConnectionService/android.telecom. Source: [Android developers FGS types (CN mirror)](https://developer.android.google.cn/develop/background-work/services/fg-service-types?hl=hu).

### Inferences
- A Play-distributed recorder that is not the default dialer cannot legitimately read the call log, cannot use Accessibility for call audio, and lacks the privileged CAPTURE_AUDIO_OUTPUT / VOICE_CALL audio source. That audio source is privileged or system-only, which is general Android knowledge not re-verified here. It is therefore limited to microphone capture (often only the user's side, or speakerphone) with a microphone FGS.
- A third-party app that becomes the default dialer (ROLE_DIALER) can get Call Log access and the phoneCall FGS. However, the Engadget quote ties the audio exemption to apps that are both default and preloaded. Whether a non-preloaded default dialer can capture downlink audio depends on the device and OEM, not on policy.
- PROCESS_OUTGOING_CALLS is deprecated (since API 29). It falls under the Call Log permission group, so the same default-handler restriction should be assumed. Its exact current policy status is not confirmed in sources.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch the live current wording of the Accessibility API policy page (answer/10964491) to confirm it is unchanged in 2026.
- No source found on Play review outcomes specifically for third-party "default dialer + recorder" apps in 2025–2026.
- The current Play declaration status of READ_PHONE_STATE (not a restricted permission, but needs a privacy-policy disclosure) is not verified.

## 2. How Cube ACR, ACR Phone and others comply: helper APKs, sideloading, Android restricted settings, and 2026 developer verification

### Takeaway
The main apps split into a Play-compliant main app and a separately distributed helper APK that holds the Accessibility service (Cube ACR Helper, NLL's ACR Phone Helper). Android 13+ "Restricted settings" and Android 15's Enhanced Confirmation Mode add user friction for sideloaded Accessibility services. Since 30 September 2026, Android developer verification is enforced in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand, with a global rollout planned for 2027. Helper APKs then need to come from a verified, registered developer, or users must use ADB or the "advanced flow" with a waiting period.

### Cited Findings
- Cube ACR Helper is a companion APK that restores recording on Android 9+ after the Play Accessibility ban. The main Cube ACR app stays on Play. On Android 13+ users must "allow restricted settings" and enable "Use Cube ACR app connector". The Helper is downloaded as an APK from outside Play. The latest APKMirror build is 1.0.17, targeting Android 13. Source: [Cube ACR Helper page](https://cubeacr.app/helper.html), [APKMirror Cube ACR Helper](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/cube-systems/cube-acr-helper).
- Cube's guide also says the connector "can no longer be requested through accessibility" in some setups, affecting caller detection and recording the other party on some Android versions. VoIP recording depends on the device. Source: [Cube ACR Helper page](https://cubeacr.app/helper.html) (via search summary).
- NLL: apps using Accessibility for call recording, "including ACR Phone are no longer permitted to use Accessibility Service API to record calls on their Google Play Store version". The ACR Phone Helper (APH) restores recording via Accessibility. It is needed only on Android 10+, is distributed via nllStore/APKMirror (latest listed build June 2024), and may need loudspeaker on some phones. Source: [NLL Future of ACR](https://nllapps.com/apps/acr/future), [NLL Android 9 note](https://nllapps.com/apps/acr/android9.htm), [APKMirror APH](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/nll-apps/acr-phone-helper-aph).
- Truecaller removed its Accessibility-based free recording after the policy: "as per the updated Google Developer Program policies, we are unable to offer call recording any longer." Source: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/truecaller-call-recording-google-play-policy).
- Restricted settings (Android 13): Accessibility and notification-listener services of apps installed via non-session installers (browsers, file managers) are blocked until the user taps "Allow restricted settings" from the app info page. Source: [CleanBrowsing guide](https://cleanbrowsing.org/support/mobile/disable-restricted-settings-android), [dev.to](https://dev.to/ahmmedrejowan/android-13s-restricted-setting-a-stronghold-against-malicious-apps-1ea9).
- Android 15 extends the restricted list to Accessibility, Notification listener, Device admin, Display over other apps, Usage access, and the Dialer and SMS roles for sideloaded apps. Enhanced Confirmation Mode adds a further dialog. Source: [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2024/09/12/android-15-sideloaded-apps-restrictions/), [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/android-15-enhanced-confirmation-mode-3436697).
- Developer verification: Google's developer page says protections begin for users installing apps from participating stores in select regions on certified devices. Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand come first (requirements from 30 Sep 2026), with a global rollout in 2027. Source: [developer.android.com/developer-verification](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification), [Android Developers Blog, Jun 2026](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/06/android-developer-verification.html), [Technology.org timeline](https://www.technology.org/2026/06/19/android-developer-verification-2026-timeline/).
- Verification opened to all developers in Play Console and the new Android Developer Console in March 2026. An "Android Developer Verifier" system app was announced to ship to phones. Source: [Android Developers Blog, Mar 2026](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/03/android-developer-verification-rolling-out-to-all-developers.html), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/03/30/android-developer-verifier-app/), [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/03/31/android-developer-verification-requirement/).
- 99% of Play apps were registered automatically. Off-Play developers must verify their identity and register package names. Reported fee: $25 for full distribution, plus a free "limited distribution" hobbyist account capped at 20 devices (third-party reporting). Source: [Android Developers Blog, Jun 2026](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/06/android-developer-verification.html), [Doply](https://www.doply.io/en/blog/android-developer-verification-explained).
- The "advanced flow" for installing unverified apps (with a waiting period reported as one day) began rolling out gradually in August 2026. ADB installs are exempt. Source: [9to5Google, Aug 2026](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/18/google-gradually-rolling-out-androids-advanced-sideloading-ahead-of-developer-verification/), [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/google-developer-verification-sideloaded-apps), [Northeast Times](https://northeasttimes.com/2026/07/26/google-plans-to-lock-down-android-app-sideloading-by-late-2026/).
- Participating stores in the first wave reportedly include Google Play, Samsung Galaxy Store, HONOR App Market, OPPO App Market, Palm Store (Transsion), vivo V-Appstore and Xiaomi GetApps. Source: [Neoteo](https://www.neoteo.com/en/google-starts-android-developer-verification-protections-in-four-countries) (secondary; confirm on Google's page).

### Inferences
- Verification is about developer identity, not content policy. A helper APK from a verified developer should still install outside Play, though verification removes anonymity and lets Google block package names tied to malware. Google could in principle refuse or revoke a registration for an Accessibility-abusing helper, but no source shows Google doing this to call-recording helpers.
- For an Israel-targeted app, verification enforcement is expected to apply globally from 2027. Until then, Israeli users are not in the first-wave regions.
- The helper APK model stacks friction: unknown-sources prompt, then restricted settings, then ECM (Android 15+), then possibly the advanced-flow wait (2027+ for unverified developers). Registering as a verified developer avoids only the last of these.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Cube or NLL have registered their helper APKs under developer verification, or made public statements about it.
- No source covered Android 16 changes to restricted settings or ECM.
- No confirmed source on F-Droid's stance on call-recorder helpers. Whether F-Droid apps are affected by verification (F-Droid publicly objected in 2025) was not researched here.

## 3. Other stores: Samsung Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps

### Takeaway
No public store-specific rules on call recording were found for Galaxy Store, AppGallery or GetApps. Galaxy Store and GetApps are reported as participating stores in Google's developer verification, so distributing there does not bypass it.

### Cited Findings
- Galaxy Store and Xiaomi GetApps are listed among the stores participating in Android developer verification (first wave). Source: [Neoteo](https://www.neoteo.com/en/google-starts-android-developer-verification-protections-in-four-countries), [Android Authority (via search summary)](https://www.androidauthority.com/android-sideloading-changes-timeline-3679204/).
- OEM dialers (Samsung, Xiaomi) can record calls natively because they are preloaded default dialers outside the Play Accessibility rule. Source: [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/google_to_kill_callrecording_apps_on_play_store_on_may_11-news-54039.php), [SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/android-call-recording-apps-wont-work-galaxy-phone-may-11/).
- NLL says users "may need to install APH from other app stores" to restore recording. Source: [NLL](https://nllapps.com/apps/acr/android9.htm).

### Inferences
- Installs from a store that uses session-based installation are treated as not sideloaded for restricted settings, so a store-installed helper avoids that extra prompt. This follows from how restricted settings are defined in [dev.to](https://dev.to/ahmmedrejowan/android-13s-restricted-setting-a-stronghold-against-malicious-apps-1ea9).

### Gaps
- No Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery or Xiaomi GetApps review guidelines on Accessibility-based recording were found. Huawei AppGallery's participation in verification is not mentioned (Huawei devices without GMS are not "certified Android", so verification likely does not apply; this is unverified).

## 4. Legal: consent laws (US, EU/GDPR, UK, Israel), announcement features, data storage and AI

### Takeaway
Israel, the UK (personal use) and most US states and federal law allow a participant to record without the other party's consent. Eleven US states, plus nuanced Connecticut and Michigan, require all-party consent, and for interstate calls the stricter rule is usually followed. Personal recordings mostly sit under GDPR's household exemption, but sharing or business use triggers full data-protection duties. Google's own Pixel recorder plays a non-bypassable announcement. An optional announcement or beep, plus clear consent guidance, is the safe design default.

### Cited Findings
- Israel: under the Secret Monitoring (Wiretapping) Law 5739-1979, a participant may record a conversation they take part in without notifying the others. Third-party interception without lawful authority is a crime punishable by up to five years. Use or disclosure of unlawfully obtained content is also prohibited. Source: [RecordingLaw Israel](https://www.recordinglaw.com/world-laws/world-recording-laws/israel-recording-laws/), [Hamichlol: חוק האזנת סתר](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%90%D7%96%D7%A0%D7%AA_%D7%A1%D7%AA%D7%A8), [Knesset law PDF](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/9/law/9_lsr_208328.PDF).
- Israel: civil service regulations bar a civil servant from recording another civil servant without consent, with exceptions such as exposing corruption. This is stricter than the Wiretap Law. Source: [law.co.il](https://www.law.co.il/en/news/2022/01/13/civil-service-regulations-forbid-wiretapping-civil-servants/).
- Israel: the Protection of Privacy Law 5741-1981 may restrict use or publication of a recording that was lawful to make. Source: [RecordingLaw Israel](https://www.recordinglaw.com/world-laws/world-recording-laws/israel-recording-laws/).
- Israel: a proposal attributed to PM Netanyahu to require all-party consent has been reported. Its date and status were unclear, with no evidence it passed. Source: [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/404655).
- US: the federal Wiretap Act is one-party consent. All-party states are commonly listed as CA, DE, FL, IL, MD, MA, MT, NV, NH, PA and WA, with CT and MI nuanced or situational. For interstate calls, sources advise following the strictest law. Source: [Rev](https://www.rev.com/blog/phone-call-recording-laws-state), [Koncert](https://support.koncert.com/support/solutions/articles/6000280354-what-is-the-two-party-consent-state-law-and-which-states-have-that-law-), [Scribbl 2026 guide](https://scribbl.co/post/do-you-have-to-tell-people-youre-recording).
- UK: recording a call you take part in for personal use is lawful under RIPA 2000 s.1(6) without notifying the other party. Personal and household recording is exempt from most UK GDPR obligations (Art. 2(2)(c)). Sharing a recording beyond personal use can need consent or a lawful basis. Businesses must inform parties and follow GDPR principles. These are secondary or vendor sources. Source: [Sonetel](https://sonetel.com/en/help/help-topics/telephony/free-call-recording/is-it-legal-to-record-calls/is-it-legal-to-record-calls-in-the-united-kingdom), [RecordingLaw UK](https://www.recordinglaw.com/world-laws/world-recording-laws/uk-recording-laws/), [Mediahawk](https://support.mediahawk.co.uk/support/solutions/articles/17000093253-call-recording-uk-gdpr).
- Pixel Phone app call recording (rolled out widely in late 2025 to Pixel 6+, and later to all Pixel countries) plays a message after a short countdown to notify everyone on the call. This is done for regulatory reasons and cannot be bypassed. A red dot indicator shows during recording, recordings are stored on-device, and availability varies by jurisdiction. Source: [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/11/13/pixel-phone-call-recording/), [MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/2025/11/18/googles-call-recording-is-launching-on-more-pixel-devices/), [Droid Tools](https://droid.tools/google-pixel-phone-app-update-brings-call-recording-and-android-auto-worldwide/).
- Cube ACR's developer advises users that laws vary and recommends informing the other party and obtaining consent. Source: [APKMirror Cube ACR Helper](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/cube-systems/cube-acr-helper).

### Inferences
- For an app aimed at Israeli users, participant recording is lawful. The app should still offer an optional announcement or beep, show in-app legal notices, and geofence or warn for all-party jurisdictions, to match Google's own posture and its "remote = unaware" framing.
- Once recordings or transcripts go to the developer's cloud or to an AI API, the developer becomes a controller or processor under GDPR and the Israeli Privacy Law, Amendment 13 (not researched here). This calls for a privacy policy, encryption at rest and in transit, retention controls and DPAs with AI vendors. Play's User Data policy also requires prominent disclosure and a privacy policy.
- On-device storage plus optional end-to-end-encrypted backup minimizes legal exposure, as Pixel does.

### Gaps
- Primary text of Germany §201 StGB and other strict EU national laws (recording the non-public spoken word without consent) was not retrieved. Treat strict-consent EU states as a risk without citation here.
- Israeli Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13 (in force 2025) obligations for apps were not researched.
- No authoritative source found on EU AI Act implications for call transcription or summaries.
- The exact wording of the Pixel announcement could not be confirmed.

## 5. Monetization and features (transcription, AI summaries) in 2025–2026 recorders

### Takeaway
The market has moved to freemium plus subscription, with AI transcription and summaries as premium hooks. OEM and first-party dialers (Pixel Call Notes, Galaxy AI) now bundle these for free, which squeezes third-party apps.

### Cited Findings
- Cube ACR reportedly charges $19.99 per year for Premium on Android and $49.99 per year on iPhone in the US, with a 7-day trial. Premium adds cloud backup, notes and no ads (aggregator data). Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/276785/Cube-ACR/), [PricingNow](https://pricingnow.com/question/cube-acr-pricing/).
- Truecaller AI Call Recorder is a Premium feature with LLM transcription, summaries and subject lines. It launched in the US and Canada (Nov 2023) and India (Feb 2024; Premium from INR 75 per month or INR 529 per year). It offers a recording button in the Truecaller dialer and a floating button on other dialers. English and Hindi were supported in India. Source: [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231108425879/en), [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/quantum-brief/truecaller-launches-ai-enabled-call-recording-in-india-with-transcription-and-call-summary-24524.htm), [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!technology/truecaller-announces-ai-powered-call-recording-feature-in-india-enn24022604380).
- Google Pixel Call Notes uses on-device Gemini Nano to transcribe and summarize calls and is distinct from basic Call Recording. It is expanding to more countries (Australia, Canada, India, Ireland as beta). Source: [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/11/13/pixel-phone-call-recording/), [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/google-makes-it-possible-to-record-phone-calls-on-the-pixel-9-series-using-the-call-notes-feature_id161669), [Droid Tools](https://droid.tools/google-pixel-phone-app-update-brings-call-recording-and-android-auto-worldwide/).

### Inferences
- Common feature set: auto or manual recording rules (all calls, unknown numbers, contacts), cloud backup (Drive or Dropbox), notes and tags, search, transcription, AI summaries and action items, and a spam blocker or dialer. Summaries are the main subscription differentiator.
- On-device transcription (Whisper-class models) is a privacy selling point compared with cloud LLMs, especially under GDPR and Israeli law.

### Gaps
- Hebrew transcription quality and availability in competing recorders was not researched.
- No verified 2026 pricing for ACR Phone or Truecaller Premium.
- Exactly how Truecaller's AI recorder captures audio on Android (a recording-line conference call, as widely reported, rather than local capture) was not confirmed in sources found.
